Methodology & data

  • Cost is the measured billed cost per Intelligence Index task including input, reasoning and answer tokens — not a per-token price.
  • Speed is the measured end-to-end time per task (output speed, time to first token, reasoning length and provider queueing combined).
  • Index eras are incomparable: scores and costs measured under different index compositions are never mixed — pick one era above.
  • The green “Preferred corner” box marks the cheaper/faster/smarter octant of the full era range (split at the log midpoint for cost and speed, mid-range for intelligence) — a relative guide, not an optimum. It disappears when the full era range collapses.
  • Data: Artificial Analysis measurements via the CatalystNeuro LLM Frontier updater (methodology, code, BSD-3-Clause). Upstream data is governed by Artificial Analysis terms. See README for limits.
  • Deals (off by default) adds separately labeled estimate points next to the measured ones: Meta Muse Spark Contributor repricing for Muse Spark 1.3 (xhigh) and 1.2 (xhigh) — Standard rates $0.15/$1.25/$4.25 perM vs Contributor $0.002/$0.10/$0.20 (cached/input/output), blended at an assumed 7:2:1 mix (.0414/.78, no per-task token counts published) — and OpenCode Go quota-equivalent estimates ($10/mo over the $15/$30/$60 tier at your quota-use %; rolling 5h 20% / weekly 50% / monthly 100%). Estimates inherit benchmark intelligence/speed (not provider measurements), assume benchmark cost meters at Go rates (never exact parity), assume the full budget is spent on the modeled tier, and ignore peak/off-peak and context-tier price splits. Contributor pricing allows training use; Muse Spark (max) is Standard-only and stays measured-only. Curated by hand in src/deals.js from the Meta pricing/rate-limits, reasoning, models and OpenCode Go docs (reviewed 2026-09-24; not auto-refreshed — re-check the sources; DeepSeek V4.1 Flash uses the active 4x promo quota until 2026-09-28 UTC, then the base tier).
